How JOIN2026 pays, and the 1 dollar Send floor: the cashback is paid by the code owner, not by Fomo, as an in-app USDC Send: every Sunday once 25 USDC has accrued, and on the 1st of each month from 1 USDC. Nothing is needed from you beyond adding the code before your first trade (cashback terms).
What JOIN2026 does not change: the fixed amounts on Solana trades under 190 USDC and the lowered 0.05% rate on select major tokens (Help Center #14436214), and perps. Perps are charged up to 0.05% of position size by Fomo plus Hyperliquid's own fee (Help Center #15453645), with no referral discount and no cashback, and they are not available to US persons. The 0.3825% net figure applies to spot trades charged at the percentage rate: Solana trades of 190 USDC and up, and trades on the other chains, where the network fee is paid on top.
